Copyright & Content Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page covers what you're allowed to post here, and what to do if something posted on beyondefinition shouldn't be — because it infringes your copyright, uses your likeness without permission, or otherwise shouldn't be here.

beyondefinition is owned and run by a single individual, operating under the name "Selrick." There's no company, team, or other owners behind it — just one person, which is also who reviews any report or request sent to the contact address on this page.

1. Only post what's yours to share

You may only upload or attach an image, gif, video, song, or any other piece of media to this site if you own it, or you have the rights or permission to share it here. This applies everywhere you can attach media — a thought, a diary entry, or a reply. Don't upload someone else's copyrighted photos, art, video, or music, and don't upload private photos or recordings of other people without their consent.

2. Never allowed, no exceptions

Sexual content involving minors is never tolerated in any form and will be removed and reported as required by law. This applies regardless of how it's framed or described.

3. Music previews

The "add a song" feature searches Apple's public iTunes Search API and attaches the official ~30-second preview clip Apple already provides for that track. The site does not download, host a copy of, modify, or otherwise redistribute the song file itself — the preview streams directly from Apple's servers through a normal audio player each time it's played. If Apple's API or preview terms change, this feature should be re-reviewed against their current terms.

4. Reporting copyright infringement or content that shouldn't be here

If you believe something posted on this site infringes your copyright, uses your image or likeness without permission, or otherwise shouldn't be here, you can ask for it to be reviewed and removed. Please include:

  • What the content is and, if it's a copyright claim, a description of the original work it infringes
  • Where it is on the site — for example, "the public thoughts wall," "the room," or a specific diary — plus roughly when you saw it
  • A screenshot, if you're able to take one, since posts here don't have individual public links
  • Your contact information
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorized, and (if you're the rights holder or their agent) a statement that the information you provided is accurate

Send reports to: selrickbeyondefinition@gmail.com.

Note: the Philippines has its own notice-and-takedown framework under the Intellectual Property Code and the E-Commerce Act, separate from the U.S. DMCA. This process is modeled on common notice-and-takedown practice for clarity, but hasn't been reviewed against Philippine IP law specifically — worth a quick check with a local lawyer if this site grows or a real dispute comes up.

5. What happens after a report

Reports are reviewed personally by the site owner, as promptly as reasonably possible — this is a manual, human process, not an automated one, since it's a one-person site. Content may be removed or hidden while a report is being looked into. There's no guaranteed response time, and the site owner may decline reports that are incomplete, made in bad faith, or don't describe an actual issue.

If you posted something that was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can also write to the same address to ask for it to be reconsidered.

6. Repeat or clear infringement

The site owner may remove content, and may restrict or deny access to the site's posting features, for anyone who repeatedly posts infringing or rights-violating content.

7. No guarantee

This policy describes the process the site aims to follow — it doesn't guarantee that every instance of infringing content will be caught, that reports will always be resolved a particular way, or that this site is immune from legal claims related to user-posted content.
