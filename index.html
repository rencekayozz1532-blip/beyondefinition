Selrick

(noun / name)

also spelled as a schizophrenic human being.

Pronunciation: /SEL-rik/ (sel-rik; the recording says otherwise)

Meaning (revised)

A name, and the one person who answers to it — though not always in the same voice. Heard clearly by some in a room and not at all by others. The entry has been corrected more than once, by the same hand each time, sounding a little different at each correction.

Example (unattributed)

"Selrick was known before the name was heard, and introduced afterwards as someone new."
