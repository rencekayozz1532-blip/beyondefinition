Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page explains, in plain terms, what information beyondefinition collects and how it's used. The short version: the site mostly just stores what you choose to write or upload, plus a small amount of technical data needed to keep it running.

beyondefinition is owned and run by a single individual, operating under the name "Selrick." There's no company, team, or other owners behind it — just one person, which is also who reviews any report or request sent to the contact address on this page.

1. What this site collects

  • Content you submit — thoughts, diary entries, room posts and replies, any photo/gif/video/song you attach, and any name or alias you choose to use.
  • Device-local data — the site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember things like a draft in progress, whether you've liked something, a chosen display name, or a login session for a diary you've unlocked. This stays on your device and isn't something the site reads unless you submit it.
  • Basic technical data — the site's hosting provider and backend provider (see below) may log standard technical information as part of serving the page and handling requests, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. This site does not run its own separate analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Push notification data, only if you opt in — if you choose to turn on notifications, your browser creates a push subscription (an endpoint and encryption keys) that's stored so the site can send you an alert. Nothing is sent unless you've explicitly turned this on, and you can turn it off in your browser.

(This list reflects what's actually in the site's code today — no analytics or ad trackers were found. If that changes later, update this section to match.)

2. How this data is used

To display the content you and others post, to operate features like likes and notifications, to respond to copyright or content reports (see the Copyright & Content Policy), and to keep the site reasonably free of abuse (for example, basic rate-limiting on posting).

3. Who else sees it

This site runs on top of a few outside services, each of which processes data on the site's behalf and has its own privacy policy and terms:

  • Supabase — the database, file storage, authentication, and backend functions that power the site.
  • Render — the hosting provider serving the site's pages.
  • Google Fonts — loads the site's typefaces; your browser makes a request to Google's font servers, which may see your IP address.
  • Apple's iTunes Search API — used when you search for a song to attach; your search term and IP address go to Apple's servers, the same as visiting any Apple webpage.
  • Groq — powers the interactive chat/companion feature; if you message the companion, your message and the last few lines of that chat are sent to Groq's API to generate a reply.

See the Attributions page for links to each of their policies. This site does not sell personal data to anyone.

4. Cookies

As far as the site's own code is concerned, no tracking cookies are set — it uses browser local storage instead, which isn't sent to the server automatically the way a cookie is. The hosting or backend providers above may use cookies or similar technology of their own as part of running their infrastructure; check their policies for specifics.

5. How long content is kept

Posted content generally stays on the site until it's removed — by the person who posted it, where that's possible (for example, a diary owner deleting their own entries), or by the site owner, including in response to a copyright or content report.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at, and is not intended to be used by, children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and does not currently include a technical age check. If you believe a child under 13 has posted personal information here, contact the address below and it will be removed.

7. Your choices

Most fields on this site are free text — please avoid including personal information you don't want visible to others, since posts (other than code-protected diaries) are generally public. To ask about, or request removal of, something you've posted, use the contact details below.

8. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect data handled by this site and its providers, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and this policy doesn't promise otherwise.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date at the top reflects the most recent update.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data: selrickbeyondefinition@gmail.com.
